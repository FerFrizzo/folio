# Delete your Folio account

*Folio — the invoicing app for Australian small business*

You can delete your account in either of two ways.

**In the app:** open **Settings → About → Delete account** and confirm. Your
account and its data (listed below) are deleted straight away.

**By email:** write to

**[fernandofrizzo@gmail.com](mailto:fernandofrizzo@gmail.com?subject=Delete%20my%20Folio%20account)**

Use the subject line **"Delete my Folio account"** and include the email address linked to your account (if any). We will action your request within **7 days**. An emailed request also removes your usage-analytics data (see below).

---

## What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed:

- Business profile (name, ABN, address, logo)
- All clients, invoices, credit notes, and payment records
- Settings and preferences
- Line item library
- Your Firebase authentication record

## What is retained

- **Usage-analytics events** — records of which app features were used (for
  example "invoice sent"), linked to a pseudonymous account ID rather than
  your name or email, and stored with PostHog in the EU. In-app account
  deletion does not remove them. Email us (above) and we will delete them
  within 7 days; otherwise they are kept for PostHog's data-retention period
  and then removed. They never include invoice contents, amounts, client
  details, or your email address.

Apart from that, nothing is kept once your account is deleted.

Note: if you have already exported PDFs or CSV files to your device, those local copies are unaffected — we cannot delete files stored on your own device.

## Partial data deletion (optional)

You can also delete individual records at any time from within the app — invoices, credit notes, clients, and line items can each be deleted independently without deleting your account. You can stop usage analytics at any time in **Settings → Privacy → Share usage data**.
